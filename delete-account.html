Delete your TribeTab account

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In the app

Open TribeTab, go to Settings, and tap Delete my account. You need to be settled up first: your balance must be zero in every group and with every friend, and no payment can be waiting for confirmation.

What is deleted

Your sign-in (your email address), your name and your notification tokens are deleted straight away. If you own a group, ownership passes to another member; a group with nobody else in it is deleted.

What is kept, and why

Expenses and payments you shared stay in the other members' history, shown as "Deleted user", because their balances depend on them. They no longer carry your name or email.

Can't open the app?

Write to satyajitg@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.
