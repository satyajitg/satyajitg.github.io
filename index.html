TribeTab

Split group expenses without the spreadsheet or the awkward IOUs.

TribeTab is a simple Android app for groups who share costs — flatmates, trips, a weekly badminton game, anything where more than one person pays and everyone needs to know where they stand. Add what was spent, and TribeTab keeps a running, always-live tally of who paid, who owes, and who's owed.

TribeTab never moves money. It only keeps the ledger straight. When someone pays another member back — by cash, UPI, bank transfer, however — they record it in the app. A payment recorded by a group admin counts straight away (and the other person can dispute it); otherwise the other person confirms it actually happened.

Sign in with your Google account or your email, create or join a group, and start adding expenses. Balances are always read live, so nobody sees a number that's out of date.
