TribeTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what information TribeTab collects, why, and how it's used. TribeTab is a group expense-splitting app built and operated by Satyajit. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, write to satyajit@agilemania.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, only to identify your account — TribeTab does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or anything else in your Google account. You can also sign in with just your email address.

Group and expense data. The groups you create or join, the expenses you add (description, amount, currency, date, category and any notes), and the payments you record or confirm between group members.

Device information. A limited, encrypted local copy of your groups and expenses is kept on your device so the app works smoothly. Balances are never cached — they're always read fresh from the server.

How we use this information

Solely to run the app: to identify you, keep your groups and expenses in sync, and calculate who owes what within each group. We do not use your data for advertising, and TribeTab has no ad network or tracking SDK.

Who can see your data

Expenses and balances in a group are visible only to the members of that group. Nobody outside a group can see its expenses.

Where your data is stored

TribeTab's data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider, and encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers (like Supabase and Google Sign-In) that TribeTab itself runs on.

TribeTab never moves money

TribeTab is a ledger, not a payment processor. It does not initiate, hold, or transfer funds of any kind. Members settle up outside the app (cash, UPI, bank transfer, etc.) and simply record that it happened.

Your choices

You can leave any group at any time. To delete your account and the personal data associated with it, write to satyajit@agilemania.com and we will action the request promptly, subject to keeping the shared expense records other group members still rely on for their own balances.

Children's privacy

TribeTab is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
