TribeTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what information TribeTab collects, why, and how it's used. TribeTab is a group expense-splitting app built and operated by Satyajit. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, write to satyajit@agilemania.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, only to identify your account — TribeTab does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or anything else in your Google account. You can also sign in with just your email address.

Group and expense data. The groups you create or join, the expenses you add (description, amount, currency, date, category and any notes), and the payments you record or confirm between group members.

Device information. A local copy of your groups and expenses is kept on your device so the app works smoothly; your sign-in session is stored encrypted, and the copy is excluded from device backups. Balances are never cached — they're always read fresh from the server.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, your device's notification token is stored so we can tell you about new expenses, payments to confirm and groups you are added to. It is deleted when you sign out.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a crash report (device model, Android version, app version and where in the code it failed) is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it. Crash reports contain no expense amounts or group content.

How we use this information

Solely to run the app: to identify you, keep your groups and expenses in sync, and calculate who owes what within each group. We do not use your data for advertising, and TribeTab has no ad network, analytics or tracking SDK.

Who can see your data

Expenses and balances in a group are visible only to the members of that group. Nobody outside a group can see its expenses. The same applies to expenses shared with friends outside any group: only the people on them can see them. Anyone who knows the email address you sign in with can add you to a group they run or to a shared expense, which tells them you have a TribeTab account; your summary shows what you owe and are owed across everything you are part of.

Where your data is stored

TribeTab's data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider, and encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers TribeTab itself runs on: Supabase (database), Google Sign-In, and Google Firebase (notifications and crash reports).

TribeTab never moves money

TribeTab is a ledger, not a payment processor. It does not initiate, hold, or transfer funds of any kind. Members settle up outside the app (cash, UPI, bank transfer, etc.) and simply record that it happened.

Your choices

You can leave a group yourself once you are settled up, or ask its admin to remove you. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) once every balance is zero. See how account deletion works, or write to satyajit@agilemania.com to ask us to do it for you.

Children's privacy

TribeTab is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
